Common Questions

How much does cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of the space, the type of cleaning and how often you want it. We give every customer a free, custom quote, so you know the price before we start.

What is the difference between a regular clean and a deep clean?

A regular clean keeps a home that is already in good shape fresh. A deep clean goes further and is the right pick when a home has not been cleaned in a while or needs a reset. Tell us about your space and we will recommend the right one.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. We bring our own cleaning supplies and equipment.

Do you clean rentals between guests?

Yes. Airbnb and vacation rental turnovers are one of the services we offer, along with move-in and move-out cleaning for rentals and apartments.

Do you clean offices?

Yes. We offer commercial and office cleaning. Call or text with the size of the space and how often you need it.
