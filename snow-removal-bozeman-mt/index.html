What We Clear

Residential snow removal for homeowners across the Gallatin Valley. We clear the places you actually walk and drive, so you can get out the door and back home safely:

  • Driveways
  • Sidewalks and walkways
  • Decks and patios
  • Rooftops

We work with snowblowers and shovels, and we treat walking surfaces with salt and ice melt where it helps. We do not run a plow, so this service is built around residential driveways, walks and entries.

Per Visit or All Season

Pick the setup that fits. You can book snow removal one visit at a time, or sign up for a seasonal contract so you never have to think about it when the forecast turns. Routine snow removal starts once snowfall reaches 2 inches. Every property is quoted individually, and quotes are free.

Rooftop Snow Removal

Snow that stacks up on a roof sheds onto walkways and entryways, and Montana roofs hold a lot of it. We can clear rooftop snow as part of your service. We will confirm what is possible for your roof and the conditions when we put your quote together.

Common Questions

How much snow before you come out?

Routine snow removal starts at 2 inches of snowfall. If you have a situation that cannot wait for that, tell us when you call and we will talk it through.

Do you plow?

No. We clear with snowblowers and shovels, and use salt and ice melt on walking surfaces. That is a good fit for residential driveways, sidewalks, walkways, decks and patios.

Do you clear parking lots or commercial properties?

Not right now. Our snow removal is focused on residential properties.

How much does it cost?

Every property is different, so we quote each one individually. It is free, and there is no obligation. Call or text your address and what you need cleared.
